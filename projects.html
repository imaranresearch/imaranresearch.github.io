Scientific Software · Open Access CPC, 2024

SHDEM: Non-Spherical Particle DEM in LAMMPS

Developed a spherical harmonic-based DEM package inside LAMMPS to simulate non-convex, arbitrarily shaped particles — directly relevant to industrial powders that deviate significantly from the sphere assumption used in conventional DEM. Implemented contact detection algorithms for non-spherical geometries and parallelised the module with MPI and OpenMP for large-scale HPC runs. Collaborated with EPCC and Sandia National Laboratory to meet LAMMPS integration standards.

GPU-Accelerated SPH–DEM for Hydropower Turbine Design

Led simulation of a novel turbine concept for an industrial client using DualSPHysics on GPU. The model captured air–water multiphase free-surface flow and coupled fluid–solid interaction for the rotating turbine geometry via Project Chrono. This work involved direct collaboration with the client to scope deliverables and present results. Additional SPH work included gearbox lubrication modelling — oil flow dynamics around rotating gear geometries.

Energy & CCUS · Industrial Client STFC, 2024–Present

OpenFOAM Solver for CO₂ Direct Air Capture

Implemented a custom dynamic Langmuir adsorption boundary condition in OpenFOAM, coupled with scalar transport and mass transfer, to model CO₂ uptake on sorbent surfaces for direct air capture (DAC) process design. Additionally developed reactive multiphase CFD models for industrial CO₂ absorption into NaOH solvent — gas–liquid flow with reactive species transport and interfacial mass transfer. Results from both projects were used directly by UK industrial partners for process scale-up decisions.

OpenFOAM Reactive CFD Mass Transfer Gas–Liquid Multiphase CCUS
Pharmaceuticals · Industrial Pfizer, 2023–2024

DEM Calibration & Blending Optimisation for OSD Manufacturing

Developed and validated DEM simulation workflows for pharmaceutical powder blending (EDEM) and tablet compaction (Rocky) at realistic scale — up to 10M+ particles. Calibrated cohesive contact models against angle-of-repose, shear cell, and bulk density measurements. Designed HyperStudy DoE and parametric sweep workflows to map the effect of contact parameters on mixing performance, reducing the number of physical experiments per formulation. Built Python pipelines (Pandas, Scikit-learn) for data-driven model calibration, replacing manual trial-and-error fitting.

EDEM Rocky HyperStudy DoE Python DEM Calibration
HPC & GPU · Met Office Collaboration STFC, 2024–Present

GPU Porting: NEMO Ocean Model & Scientific Codebases

As part of a Met Office collaboration, ported performance-critical sections of the NEMO ocean circulation model to GPU using OpenACC — a large-scale, production-grade scientific Fortran codebase. Separately, re-implemented a vector-borne disease epidemiological model in JAX/XLA to enable GPU-accelerated parameter sweeps, substantially reducing the time required for large sensitivity analyses. Both projects involved profiling existing CPU code, identifying bottlenecks, and introducing GPU-parallel data structures.

OpenACC JAX / XLA Fortran Python GPU Code Transformation
Research Computing · Open Access Soft Matter, 2021

Self-Propelled Particle Dynamics in LAMMPS

During PhD research, extended LAMMPS in C++ with custom fixes to model self-propelled (active matter) particle systems. Developed stochastic simulation frameworks to study the emergent collective dynamics of active rod systems in soft substrates — including morphodynamics, dense suspension rheology, and substrate interaction. Simulations were validated against analytical theory and experimental observations.
